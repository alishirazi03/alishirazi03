### About Me

I am a Biosciences graduate from **COMSATS University Islamabad** working at the intersection of **wet-lab molecular genetics** and **computational genomics**. My research combines experimental bench protocols (genomic DNA extraction, primer design, TETRA-ARMS PCR) with reproducible bioinformatics pipelines, phylogenetic modeling, and biostatistics.

- **Current Focus:** Preparing applications Master's programs (2027 intake) in **Molecular Biosciences, Genetics and Computational Biology**
- **Research Interests:** Human genetic variation, membrane-transporter pathology, viral comparative genomics and metabolic drug target discovery.
